What "no website listed" means here

A place is counted as having no website listed when the websites field of its Overture record is missing, empty or holds only blank entries (588 of the places counted here have a list with only blank entries). That is a statement about the data, not about the business: a missing field does not prove the business has no website, and some listed websites are social media pages or dead links. Overture's coverage is uneven between countries, cities and categories, so compare cities with care. The counts include every kind of place Overture lists, not only commercial businesses.

Area covered: a circle of 15 km around the centre point of Kampala (706.0 km2). The source has no usable boundary polygon for this city, so the circle includes neighbouring towns and may miss outer districts.

Counted: places with an Overture confidence score of at least 0.5 that are not marked permanently closed. Not counted: 4,167 places in the same area with a lower or missing confidence score, and 0 marked permanently closed. The totals depend on this cutoff: a higher or lower threshold gives different numbers. See the methodology for the exact query.
