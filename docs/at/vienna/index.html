What "no website listed" means here

A place is counted as having no website listed when the websites field of its Overture record is missing, empty or holds only blank entries (408 of the places counted here have a list with only blank entries). That is a statement about the data, not about the business: a missing field does not prove the business has no website, and some listed websites are social media pages or dead links. Overture's coverage is uneven between countries, cities and categories, so compare cities with care. The counts include every kind of place Overture lists, not only commercial businesses.
